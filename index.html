Farmer Community Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 17, 2026

Farmer Community ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, shared, and protected when you use the Farmer Community mobile application.

By using Farmer Community, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, Farmer Community may collect or process the following types of information:

  • Account and registration information.
  • Profile information that you choose to provide.
  • Posts, comments, photos, videos, and other content you upload.
  • Messages and other communications sent through the app.
  • Marketplace information when you use marketplace features.
  • Information relating to farming activities or content you choose to share.
  • Device and technical information required to operate and maintain the app.
  • Information necessary for security, troubleshooting, and service improvement.

2. How We Use Information

Information collected through Farmer Community may be used to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Provide and operate the features of the application.
  • Display posts and other user-generated content.
  • Allow users to communicate and interact with one another.
  • Provide marketplace and community features.
  • Send important service-related notifications.
  • Improve the performance, functionality, and reliability of the app.
  • Detect, prevent, and investigate abuse, fraud, spam, or other misuse.
  • Protect the security and integrity of our services.
  • Comply with applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

3. User-Generated Content

Farmer Community allows users to voluntarily upload and share content, including posts, photos, videos, comments, farming information, and marketplace listings.

Content that you choose to publish may be visible to other users depending on the functionality and privacy settings of the application. You should avoid sharing sensitive personal information publicly.

4. Private Messages

If private messaging features are available, messages and related information may be processed as necessary to deliver communications, maintain the messaging service, protect users, and prevent misuse.

5. Third-Party Services

Farmer Community may use trusted third-party service providers to help operate the application. These services may include authentication, cloud storage, databases, notifications, analytics, security, infrastructure, and other technical services.

Third-party providers may process information as necessary to provide their services to us or as otherwise permitted by applicable law. Their handling of information may also be subject to their own privacy policies.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

However, no internet transmission, electronic storage system, or online service can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Therefore, we cannot guarantee absolute security of your information.

7. Children's Privacy

Farmer Community is not intended for children below the minimum age permitted to use the service under applicable law.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable privacy laws. If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the application, please contact us so that appropriate action can be taken.

8. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, maintain security, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

The retention period may vary depending on the type of information and the purpose for which it was collected.

9. Account and Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of their Farmer Community account and associated personal information by contacting the Farmer Community support team.

When a valid deletion request is received, we will take reasonable steps to delete or anonymize applicable information, subject to information that we are legally required or legitimately permitted to retain for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or other lawful purposes.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our services, technology, legal requirements, or privacy practices.

When changes are made, the updated Privacy Policy will be published on this page together with a revised effective date.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, privacy requests, or requests for account and data deletion, please contact the Farmer Community support team.
